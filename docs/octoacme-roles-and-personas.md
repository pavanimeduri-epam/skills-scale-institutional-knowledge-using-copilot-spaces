# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They ensure deliverables meet quality standards and acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans for each release cycle
- Establish acceptance criteria validation procedures
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify and triage quality issues
- Collaborate with Developers on test coverage and CI pipelines
- Report quality metrics and defect trends

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet quality standards
- Reduce production defects
- Enable confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA coordination meetings
- Test reports and quality dashboards
- Defect tracking and acceptance validation updates

### Interaction with existing roles
- **Developers**: Review test plans, implement tests, fix defects identified in QA
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria alignment with product requirements
- **Project Managers**: Report test coverage status and risks to timeline

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads/Architects define technical design, make architectural decisions, and manage technical risks. They provide technical guidance across projects and ensure scalability and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure code quality standards and design patterns
- Mentor Developers on technical best practices
- Advise on scalability, performance, and security trade-offs
- Document architectural decisions in ADRs (Architecture Decision Records)

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt
- Align projects with enterprise architecture standards

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review and mentoring sessions
- Risk assessments and technical trade-off analysis

### Interaction with existing roles
- **Developers**: Guide implementation, participate in design and code reviews
- **Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Identify technical risks and dependencies

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business interests, provide investment authority, and champion project success. They ensure alignment with organizational strategy and secure resources.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and success criteria
- Make investment and scope trade-off decisions
- Champion the project internally and remove obstacles
- Approve major decisions and changes
- Ensure stakeholder alignment
- Escalate issues that impact business outcomes

### Goals
- Maximize business value delivery
- Ensure executive alignment
- Enable successful project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and steering meetings
- Executive decision forums
- Investment and approval gates

### Interaction with existing roles
- **Project Managers**: Escalate risks and decisions requiring sponsor approval
- **Product Managers**: Align on success metrics and business outcomes
- **Developers**: Provide business context for prioritization decisions

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads own user experience strategy, design validation, and usability standards. They ensure deliverables are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define and validate user experience requirements
- Create design specifications, wireframes, and prototypes
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Establish design systems and accessibility standards
- Review designs and implementations for usability alignment
- Collaborate on product decisions with user-centric perspective

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive and accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support costs
- Maximize user satisfaction and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- Usability testing reports and insights
- Design system documentation and guidelines

### Interaction with existing roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on implementation details, ensure design fidelity
- **Product Managers**: Validate user needs and design alignment with product strategy
- **Project Managers**: Communicate design timelines and dependencies

---

## Operations/DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations/DevOps Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and production support. They ensure systems are reliable, scalable, and performant.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines
- Manage production environments and monitoring
- Implement automated testing and deployment processes
- Respond to incidents and ensure system reliability
- Advise on scalability, performance, and security infrastructure
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Ensure high system availability and performance
- Reduce deployment risk and cycle time
- Enable fast incident detection and recovery

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and release coordination
- Incident response and postmortems
- Infrastructure and performance metrics reviews

### Interaction with existing roles
- **Developers**: Provide deployment guidance, collaborate on CI/CD pipeline design
- **Project Managers**: Communicate deployment schedules and infrastructure dependencies
- **Technical Leads**: Advise on scalability and infrastructure trade-offs

---
